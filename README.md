# Vonit Launch

Skills that help an AI agent launch a product someone built - software, an app,
an AI tool, an open-source project or a digital product. From "it works" to "the
right people know about it, tried it, and can come back".

Most builders know how to make the product and not how to launch it. These
skills give an agent the whole path, and keep it honest: nothing goes out in the
founder's name without their approval, no spam, no invented facts.

## The skills

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `launch-plan` | **Start here.** The stages, the readiness check, owned/rented/borrowed channels, launch size |
| `understand-product` | Read the product and write a one-page brief and its product type |
| `positioning` | Who it is for, what it replaces, the one-liner and tagline |
| `launch-channels` | Where to launch by product type, platform notes (Product Hunt, Show HN, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, GitHub, app stores, directories, newsletters, press), a dated timeline |
| `launch-assets` | Landing page check, screenshots, images, demo video, copy per channel, press kit |
| `outreach` | A short personal list and individual messages, within caps |
| `launch-day` | The run sheet and how to be present all day |
| `after-launch` | Follow-ups, measuring, results post, the next launch moment |

## Use it

**In any agent that reads Agent Skills** (Claude Code, Codex and others): copy the
`skills/` folders into the agent's skills folder - for Claude Code,
`~/.claude/skills/` - and ask it to plan your launch.

**In Vonit**: say "add github.com/<this repository>". Vonit keeps every skill,
gives your agents their names, and opens the one a job needs. With
`pack.json`, Vonit also plans a launch in stages, hands images and video to
agents with the tools, offers to connect X, LinkedIn or email when a stage needs
them, and asks your approval for every post and message.

## Rules the skills keep

- Nothing is published or sent without the person's approval of the exact text
  and destination.
- No spam: no bulk or cold mass email, no bought lists, no asking for upvotes.
- No invented users, numbers, quotes, logos or press.
- Each platform's current rules win over anything written here.
