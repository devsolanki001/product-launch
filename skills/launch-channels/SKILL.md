# Launch channels and timeline

Few channels, done well, beat many done badly. Pick two or three where the
product's first users already gather, prepare each properly, and point all of
them to one place the visitor can stay (an account, a list, a community).

## Choose by product type

Start from the product type in the brief (understand-product):

| Type | First choices | Add if it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Developer tool / API / CLI | Show HN, GitHub (README, release), X | Reddit dev subs, dev newsletters, DevHunt, dev.to post |
| Open-source project | GitHub, Show HN, Reddit (relevant subs) | awesome-lists, r/selfhosted (self-hostable), Product Hunt |
| AI tool or agent | X (video demo), Product Hunt | AI directories, AI newsletters, Reddit, YouTube demo |
| B2B SaaS | LinkedIn (founder), direct outreach, Product Hunt | the role's communities and newsletters, partnerships |
| Consumer web app | Product Hunt, short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) | Reddit (where the problem is discussed), X |
| Mobile app | App store page, short video | Product Hunt, Reddit, creators in the niche |
| Digital product | The creator's own audience (email, X, LinkedIn) | marketplaces, communities, collaborations |

Then check against the person: where are they already known? An existing
audience on one platform beats a perfect strategy on a platform where nobody knows
them. Ask what they have: email list size, followers, communities they belong to.

## Timeline

Build a dated plan backwards from launch day. A typical big launch:

- **3-4 weeks before** - positioning done; launch page checked; start being
  useful in the chosen communities (comments, help - not promotion); collect
  the list of people to tell personally; create accounts where needed.
- **2 weeks before** - assets made (images, demo video, copy per channel);
  outreach drafts; a waitlist or early-access link live.
- **1 week before** - everything reviewed and approved by the person; personal
  messages to friends, users and supporters telling them the date (never asking
  them to upvote); test the sign-up path once more.
- **Launch day** - launch-day skill.
- **1-2 weeks after** - after-launch skill.

Spread channels: not everything on one day. A common shape is Product Hunt or
Show HN on launch day, then a Reddit post and a newsletter mention over the
following days, then a write-up of results a week later (people love numbers and
lessons).

Pick the day for the main channel deliberately: weekdays reach more people and
more competition; weekends are quieter on both. Check the time zone of the
audience and of the platform (Product Hunt's day starts at 12:01am Pacific).

## Output

A table: date, channel, what goes out, who prepares it, needs the person's
approval (always yes for anything published), status. Keep it in the project
so the launch can be followed over weeks.
