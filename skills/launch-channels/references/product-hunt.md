# Product Hunt

Good for: consumer apps, AI tools, productivity and maker tools, B2B tools with a
self-serve way in. Less good for: deep developer infrastructure (try Show HN),
products that need a sales call to try.

## How it works (check producthunt.com for the current rules)

- Each launch day starts at **12:01am Pacific time**; a launch runs for that day.
- Anyone can launch their own product; a "hunter" is not required.
- Product Hunt's team decides which launches are featured on the home page;
  being featured matters more than the hour you post.
- Asking for upvotes is against the rules and can get a launch penalized.
  Telling people you launched and asking for feedback is fine.

## Prepare (1-2 weeks before)

- **Name and tagline** - tagline under 60 characters, from positioning; the
  value, not the technology.
- **Thumbnail** - square, recognizable at small size (an animated GIF is
  allowed and draws the eye).
- **Gallery** - 3-6 images, the first one does the work: what it is and the
  result it gives. Use real screenshots in a simple frame with a short caption;
  check Product Hunt's current recommended image size.
- **Demo video** - under 60-90 seconds, the product doing its job, no long intro.
- **Description** - two or three short sentences.
- **Maker's first comment** - why you built it, who it is for, what is free, and a
  question you want answered. Personal, not a press release.
- **An offer** - optional: a discount or extended trial for the Product Hunt
  community.
- **Your profile** - a real maker profile, ideally with some activity on the
  site before launch day.

## Launch day

- Post right at the start of the day if you can be online for it.
- Tell your own people (email list, friends, users, communities you belong to)
  that you launched, with the link - ask for feedback, never for upvotes.
- Answer every comment, all day, in your own words.
- Watch the sign-up path; fix anything broken fast and say so in the comments.

## After

- Thank people who commented; follow up with those who asked questions.
- Move visitors into owned channels (account, list).
- A short "what we learned" post a few days later often does well on X and
  LinkedIn.
