# App stores (Apple App Store, Google Play)

For mobile apps, the store page is where most people decide.

## The store page

- **Name and subtitle / short description** - what it does, with the words people
  search for (app store search weighs them heavily).
- **Screenshots** - the first two or three decide; show the result the app gives,
  with a short caption each, not a tour of every screen.
- **Preview video** - optional, short, the app doing its main job.
- **Description** - first lines matter most; plain benefits, then features.
- **Ratings** - ask happy users for a rating inside the app at a good moment,
  using the platform's own rating prompt (rules forbid rewarding ratings).

## Getting featured

- Both stores have ways for developers to tell their editors about an upcoming
  launch or update (Apple through App Store Connect, Google through the Play
  Console); check the current process and submit a few weeks ahead.
- Editors look for quality, good design, accessibility and use of new platform
  features.

## Outside the store

- Short-form video of the app in use (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) often drives more
  downloads than anything else for consumer apps.
- Creators in the app's niche, Reddit communities where the problem is discussed,
  Product Hunt.
