# X and LinkedIn

Both work best from the founder's own account, as a person, not a logo.

## X

Good for: AI tools, developer tools, consumer apps, anything with a visual demo,
and builders who "build in public".

- **Before launch** - share the building: problems hit, decisions made, early
  screenshots. People follow a story, then show up for the launch.
- **Launch post** - one short sentence of what it does and for whom, a native video
  or GIF of it working (uploaded, not a link), and the link. A thread after it can
  explain why and how.
- **Reply** to everyone; quote-post people who tried it (with their permission if
  it is not public praise).
- **Ask** a few people who know your work to share it if they like it - personally,
  one by one, never a mass DM.

## LinkedIn

Good for: B2B SaaS, tools for a job role, anything sold to companies.

- Post from the founder's personal profile; company pages reach far fewer people.
- Lead with a personal story or a sharp observation about the problem in the first
  two lines (the rest is hidden behind "see more").
- Many find posts reach further when the link is in the first comment rather than
  in the post - test it for your account.
- Tag people only when they are genuinely part of the story.
- Reply to every comment in the first hours.

## Both

- Write each post for its platform; never paste the same text everywhere.
- Plan two or three posts over the launch week (the launch, a behind-the-scenes,
  results and lessons), not one.
