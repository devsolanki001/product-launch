# Hacker News (Show HN)

Good for: developer tools, open-source projects, technical products, anything
with a genuinely interesting technical story. The audience is skeptical and
technical; honesty and substance win, marketing language loses.

## How it works (check news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html for the current rules)

- **Show HN** is for something you made that people can try: a running product, a
  repository, a demo. Not for landing pages, sign-up-only waitlists, fundraising
  news or plain blog posts.
- Title starts with `Show HN:` followed by the name and a plain, specific
  description. No superlatives, no exclamation marks, no clickbait.
- Do not ask anyone to upvote, and do not share the link asking for votes -
  vote rings are detected and penalized.
- Launch HN is a separate format for Y Combinator companies.

## Prepare

- Make it trivially easy to try: no sign-up wall if possible, or a demo account,
  or a one-command install. A README that shows what it does in the first screen.
- Write a first comment: who you are, why you built it, how it works technically,
  what is not done yet, what feedback you want. Plain and honest about limits.
- Be ready to be online for several hours after posting.

## On the day

- Post when you can stay and answer (many post on a weekday US morning).
- Answer every comment with substance, including critical ones - thank people,
  agree where they are right, explain where they are wrong, never argue.
- If the post does not get traction, do not repost it the same day. A later,
  meaningfully improved version can be shown again.

## After

- Fix what people found; reply when it is fixed.
- A write-up ("what we learned launching on HN") can go to your blog and other
  channels later.
