# Launch assets

Every asset has one job: show the product doing its job for the right person, in
seconds. Real product, real screens, no invented numbers, logos or quotes.

## 1. The landing page (check it before anything else)

Every channel sends people here. Check, and list fixes:

- First screen: the one-liner (positioning), who it is for, one clear action
  (try it, sign up, install), and a picture or video of the product working.
- Proof, if real: users, quotes with permission, numbers, logos with permission.
- How it works in three steps; price or "free to start".
- Fast, works on a phone, the sign-up path works end to end.
- A way to stay in touch for those not ready (email signup).
- Analytics on, so the launch can be measured.

## 2. Screenshots and images

- Take real screenshots of the moments that show value (the result, not the
  settings page). Clean test data that looks real but is clearly sample data -
  never another person's private data.
- Frame them simply (a device or browser frame, a plain background) with a short
  caption that states the benefit.
- Sizes: make each in the size its channel asks for (Product Hunt gallery,
  X/LinkedIn link images, app store screenshots, Open Graph image for link
  previews) - check the current sizes.
- Images generated with an image tool are for backgrounds, illustrations and
  covers - never to fake product screens or results.

## 3. Demo video (the most useful single asset)

- 30-90 seconds. The product doing its job from the first second; no long intro,
  no logo animation.
- Script: the problem in one line (5s) → the product solving it, shown, not told
  (45-60s) → the result and the link (5-10s).
- Record the real product (screen recording), add captions (most people watch
  without sound), a voice-over if the person wants (their own voice, or say
  plainly that a generated voice is used).
- Export a short GIF of the key moment for places that autoplay.
- Make a vertical cut (9:16) for short-video platforms when the product is
  consumer-facing.

## 4. Copy for each channel

From the positioning, write each separately (never the same text everywhere):
Product Hunt tagline, description and maker comment; Show HN title and first
comment; Reddit post per subreddit; X post and thread; LinkedIn post; launch
email to the person's list; a changelog / blog announcement. Plain words, the
user's own vocabulary, one call to action each.

## 5. Press kit (a single page or folder)

One-paragraph description, the founder's short bio and photo (theirs), logo in
light and dark, screenshots, the demo video, key facts (launch date, price,
links), a contact. Only real facts.

## Making them with agents (in Vonit)

Hand each asset to the agent with the tools for it - an agent with a browser and
image tools for screenshots and frames, one with video tools (ffmpeg, a screen
recording) for the demo cut and GIF, a writing agent for copy. Every asset is a
draft until the person approves it. Keep the approved versions together for the
launch day.
